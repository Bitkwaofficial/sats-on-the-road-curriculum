# Receiving Bitcoin at your shop

A short guide for you, the shop owner. Keep it near the till. You do not need to
be a Bitcoin expert. You only need to do a few simple steps, and you can ask your
local ambassador any time.

You are in control of your own money. The sats go straight to your wallet. No one
in between can hold them for you or take them.

## How to receive a payment

1. Open your wallet app, `[primary wallet]`, on your phone.
2. Tap **Receive**. Show the QR code on your screen, or use the printed QR on
   your counter sign.
3. The customer opens their own wallet, taps **Send** or **Scan**, and points
   their camera at your QR.
4. For a fixed price, type the amount first so their phone fills it in. For a tip
   jar or open amount, let them type it.
5. Wait a few seconds. A ⚡ Lightning payment usually lands almost at once.

## How to check it arrived

1. Your wallet shows a **confirmation** and a small sound or tick.
2. The new balance goes up by the amount you were paid.
3. If you are not sure, look at the transaction list in your wallet. The payment
   shows there with the time.
4. If nothing shows after a minute, ask the customer to try again. Money is not
   lost. A payment that does not complete simply does not leave their wallet.

Tip: for larger sales, wait for the confirmation on your own screen before you
hand over the goods.

## How to cash out, if you want to

You do not have to cash out. Many shops keep some sats and spend them with
suppliers or other Bitcoin shops. If you do want local money, you have choices.

- You can pay a supplier who also takes Bitcoin, and skip cashing out.
- You can swap sats for `[local currency]` through the options listed for your
  country.

See **[cash-out options for your country](cash-out-options.md)** for the apps and
methods, and the notes on each one.

## Good habits

- Keep your recovery words (the seed phrase) written on paper, somewhere safe and
  private. They are the only backup of your money. Never type them into a website
  and never share them with anyone, not even someone claiming to help.
- Bitcoin's price in `[local currency]` can go up or down. If that worries you,
  swap to local money soon after each sale.
- When in doubt, ask your Sats On The Road ambassador. That is what they are
  there for.
