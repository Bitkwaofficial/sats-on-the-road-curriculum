# Merchant onboarding checklist

Use this at the stall, with one merchant at a time. Work top to bottom. The whole
thing takes about 15 to 20 minutes. Go at the merchant's pace and keep it calm.

## Before you start

- [ ] Confirm the merchant has a smartphone that can install apps, or note they
      need a feature-phone option (`[primary wallet]` or Machankura over USSD).
- [ ] Check they have a few minutes free and are not mid-sale.
- [ ] Explain in one sentence what you are doing: helping them take Bitcoin
      payments that go straight to their own phone.
- [ ] Have the signage ready: poster, table stand, stickers.

## Set up receiving

- [ ] Help them install the agreed wallet, `[primary wallet]`.
- [ ] Walk through creating the wallet. Let them tap the buttons themselves.
- [ ] If the wallet shows recovery words, help them write the words on paper.
      Tell them never to type the words into a website or share them.
- [ ] Show them the **Receive** screen and their QR code.

## Test payment

- [ ] Send a tiny test payment to their wallet (a few sats) so they see it land.
- [ ] Point out the confirmation tick and the new balance.
- [ ] Have them send a tiny amount back to you so they practise paying too.
- [ ] Show where past payments appear in the transaction list.

## Show cash-out

- [ ] Open **[cash-out-options.md](cash-out-options.md)** and show the options
      for this country.
- [ ] Remind them cashing out is optional, and that the price in `[local currency]`
      can move up or down.
- [ ] Mark any option you could not confirm today as still to verify.

## Leave signage

- [ ] Help them paste their own payment QR into the poster and table stand.
- [ ] Place the table stand where customers can see it.
- [ ] Put a sticker on the door or counter.
- [ ] Write the shop name, and the local-language line, on the poster.

## Make the first real sale

- [ ] You, the ambassador, buy something small and real from the merchant now.
- [ ] Pay for it in sats from your own wallet to the merchant's QR, at the real price.
- [ ] Have the merchant confirm the sats arrived and the balance went up.
- [ ] Point out that the sats are now theirs, in their own wallet, with no one in between.
- [ ] This first real payment is the goal of the visit. Do not skip it.

## Record it

- [ ] Note the shop name, town, date, and wallet used.
- [ ] Note whether the test payment worked and any follow-up needed.
- [ ] Add this merchant to the visit log for the monthly report:
      **[../playbook/monthly-report-template.md](../playbook/monthly-report-template.md)**.
- [ ] Thank them, and leave the ambassador contact so they can ask questions later.
