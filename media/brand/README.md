# Brand files

The logo and ready-made cards for *Sats On The Road*, and how partners may use
them. All brand material here is licensed [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE-CONTENT.md).

## Using the logo

Partners are welcome to use the Sats On The Road logo to show a link with the
project. A few rules keep it recognisable:

- **Keep its clear space.** Leave an open margin around the logo. Do not crowd it
  with text, other logos or busy backgrounds.
- **Do not recolour it.** Keep the orange `#F7931A` and near-black `#0D0D0D`. Do
  not tint, gradient or re-draw the mark.
- **Do not stretch or distort it.** Scale it evenly. Do not squash, skew, rotate or
  add effects like shadows or outlines.
- **Keep it legible.** Do not place it so small or so low-contrast that it cannot be
  read.

If you are unsure, ask before publishing.

## Which logo file to use

| File | Use it for |
|------|------------|
| [`sotr-logo.png`](./sotr-logo.png) | The full logo on a white background. Simple print, documents, light flat surfaces. |
| [`sotr-logo-transparent.png`](./sotr-logo-transparent.png) | No background. Place over light colours and photos. |
| [`sotr-logo-on-dark.png`](./sotr-logo-on-dark.png) | On a cream chip. Use on dark backgrounds and in video. |

### Vehicle magnets and banners

- **Vehicle magnets:** use [`sotr-logo-transparent.png`](./sotr-logo-transparent.png)
  on a light-coloured vehicle, or
  [`sotr-logo-on-dark.png`](./sotr-logo-on-dark.png) on a dark vehicle, so the mark
  stays readable against the paint.
- **Banners:** use [`sotr-logo-transparent.png`](./sotr-logo-transparent.png) on a
  light banner, or [`sotr-logo-on-dark.png`](./sotr-logo-on-dark.png) on a dark
  banner. Keep the clear space around it and do not stretch it to fill the width.
