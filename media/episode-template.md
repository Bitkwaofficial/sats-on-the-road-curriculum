## Structure

Keep this exact order and these timings. The running clock is a guide; the whole
episode should land between 12 and 15 minutes.

1. **Cold open in a market** (30 s), start 0:00. A strong, real moment from the
   stop, then the title card over the first shot. Use
   [`brand/title-card.html`](./brand/title-card.html) (fill in country / city).
2. **The problem with local money, told by one person** (2 min), start 0:30. One
   trader or neighbour, in their own words: rising prices, blocked transfers, money
   that loses value. One voice, not a lecture.
3. **Wallet setup moment** (2 min), start 2:30. Someone setting up their first
   self-custodial wallet. Show the screen and their reaction. This is the heart of
   the episode.
4. **Merchant's first sale** (2 min), start 4:30. A merchant takes a real ⚡
   Lightning payment. Both phones, the QR scan, the confirmation. Keep it real.
5. **Ambassador training glimpse** (1 min), start 6:30. A short look at local
   ambassadors being trained to teach and onboard others after the team leaves.
6. **Activist or journalist, voice only if requested** (2 min), start 7:30. Why
   money that cannot be frozen matters to them. Protect identity: use voice only,
   or blur, whenever the person asks. Consent first, always.
7. **Country workshop** (1 min), start 9:30. The last-day room: ambassadors,
   merchants, partners, activists and journalists together, the local community
   taking over.
8. **What happens next** (1 min), start 10:30. What the community carries forward,
   and where the road goes next.
9. **Credits with partners** (start about 11:30). The end card, the partners, the
   team, the licence and the music credit. See the sections below.

## Branding (carry the logo)

Every episode should read as Sats On The Road at a glance. Use the one logo file,
[`brand/sotr-logo.png`](./brand/sotr-logo.png), throughout:

- **Title card:** [`brand/title-card.html`](./brand/title-card.html) (fill in
  country / city), in the cold open.
- **Brand bug:** a small logo in a bottom corner, kept on screen through the whole
  episode at low opacity so it never blocks faces or captions. Use the
  [lower-third](./brand/lower-third.html) for name and role captions.
- **End card:** [`brand/end-card.html`](./brand/end-card.html), the logo, the
  tagline, the website, and the /join QR, in the credits.
- **Thumbnail:** include the logo on the episode thumbnail.

Keep the logo clear-space clean (do not stretch, recolour or crowd it), and keep
the orange #F7931A and near-black #0D0D0D brand colours for captions and cards.
