## Money and why ours fails

- What did [local currency] buy 5 years ago versus today?
- Money we do not control can be frozen, printed, or lose value
- There is another option

---

## What Bitcoin is, in plain words

- Digital money, fixed supply (21 million, no one can print more)
- Sent person to person, no one in charge
- Not a getrich scheme. The price moves, that is normal

---

## Your own wallet (the core)

1. Install the wallet
2. Create it
3. **Write your seed phrase on paper**
4. Receive a few sats
5. Send a few to your neighbour

## One real payment ⚡

- Buy something small and real, in sats
- The merchant checks it arrived
- That is Lightning: fast, cheap, person to person

---

## Stay safe

- Never share your seed phrase
- If someone asks for it, it is a scam
- Keep savings separate from spending
- Back up your seed in two safe places
