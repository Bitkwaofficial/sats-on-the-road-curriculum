# One-session version (2 hours)

**Duration:** 2 hours
**Audience:** Everyone. A market stall, a school class, a community hall.
**Use it when** there is no time for a full day. This is the session the team ran
in Lomé.

The goal is simple: every person leaves with their own wallet, a seed phrase
written down, one real Lightning payment made, and something real bought in sats.
Everything else is a bonus.

## Objectives

By the end, each person can:

1. Say in one sentence what Bitcoin is and why it matters here.
2. Hold their own wallet (self-custody) and has written down their seed phrase.
3. Receive and send sats over Lightning at least once.
4. Buy something real from a merchant in sats.
5. Know the golden rule: never share your seed phrase.

## Materials

- Phones (the learners' own, where possible), and a feature-phone option.
- A little float of sats to seed first payments.
- Printed [handout](./handout.md), translated.
- One merchant nearby, or a volunteer acting as one.
- Slides ([`slides.md`](./slides.md)) if a screen is available (optional).

## Minute-by-minute

**0-20, Money and why ours fails.**
Ask what the [local currency] bought five years ago versus today. Land inflation in
one clear example. Money we do not control can be frozen, printed, or lose value.
Keep it to stories and one number, not a lecture.
(Condensed from [Session 1](../day-1/session-1-money).)

**20-40, What Bitcoin is, in plain words.**
Digital money no one can print more of (fixed 21 million), sent person to person,
with no one in charge. Not a get-rich scheme. The price moves; that is normal.
(Condensed from [Session 2](../day-1/session-2-what-is-bitcoin).)

**40-100, Wallet setup and first sats (the core).**
Everyone installs a self-custodial wallet, creates it, and **writes the seed phrase
on paper**. Stress: never photograph it, never type it into a website, never share
it. Confirm the backup in the app. Then each person receives a few sats and sends a
few to a neighbour. Helpers move through the room. Feature-phone users use Lightning
over USSD with Machankura.
(Condensed from [Session 3](../day-1/session-3-wallet-setup).)

**100-120, Buy something real.**
Each person buys something small and real from the merchant in sats, or pays the
volunteer merchant. Show how the merchant checks it arrived, and that the sats go
straight to the merchant's own wallet.
(Condensed from [Sessions 4-5](../day-1/session-4-lightning).)

Close in the last minute: never share your seed; if someone asks for it, it is a
scam; keep savings separate from spending; back up your seed in two safe places.
Invite the keen ones to the full two-day training to become ambassadors, and point
to satsontheroad.africa and the [handout](./handout.md).

## Common questions

- **"Is it real money?"** Yes. You just sent and received it, and bought something.
- **"What if I lose my phone?"** Your seed phrase restores your wallet. That is why
  we wrote it down.
- **"Can I get rich?"** This is about using and holding money you control, not a
  scheme. The price can fall. Never risk money you need.

## Tips from the field

- Recruit two or three local helpers before you start; 60 minutes of setup needs
  hands moving through the room.
- Pre-load the float into one phone so you can send starter sats quickly.
- Line up the merchant before you begin, so the final 20 minutes is a real sale.
- Weak signal happens. Have an offline fallback and keep amounts tiny.
