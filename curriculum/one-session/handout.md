# Handout, Bitcoin in two hours

Keep this page. It is everything from today in one place.

## What you learned

- **Money we do not control** can be frozen, printed, or lose value over time.
- **Bitcoin** is digital money with a fixed supply (21 million), sent person to
  person, with no one in charge.
- **Sats** are the small unit: 100,000,000 sats = 1 bitcoin. You use sats.
- **Lightning** makes small payments fast and cheap.

## Your wallet

- You installed `[primary wallet]` and created your own wallet (self-custody).
- You wrote your **seed phrase** on paper. This is the backup of your whole wallet.

### Protect your seed phrase

- Never photograph it. Never type it into a website. Never share it with anyone.
- Keep it on paper (or metal) in two safe places.
- If your phone is lost or broken, the seed phrase brings your money back.

## The golden rule

**If anyone asks for your seed phrase, it is a scam.** Every time. No wallet,
no exchange, no "support agent", no donor ever needs it.

## Stay safe

- Keep savings separate from everyday spending.
- Ignore "double your Bitcoin" and guaranteed returns.
- Install wallet apps only from official sources.
- The price can fall. Never put in money you need to live on.

## Do this today

1. Check your seed phrase is written down and safe.
2. Send a few sats to a friend, and have them send some back.
3. Find one shop that will let you pay in sats.
