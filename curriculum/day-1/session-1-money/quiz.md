# Quiz, Session 1: Money and why ours fails

Five questions. This is for the ambassador badge, not a pass or fail test. Answer key is at the bottom.

## 1. What is money, in the simplest terms?
A. A thing that is valuable all by itself
B. A tool for trade that works because everyone agrees to accept it
C. Something only banks are allowed to make
D. A kind of gold that never loses value

## 2. Which of these is one of the three jobs of money?
A. Store of value
B. Growing on trees
C. Making you automatically rich
D. Melting in the sun

## 3. "Fiat money" means money that is:
A. Backed by gold you can collect at a bank
B. Money by decree, because the government says it is money
C. Only made of metal
D. Impossible to spend

## 4. What happens when a lot more money is created but the goods stay the same?
A. Everyone becomes richer
B. Prices tend to rise and each note buys less
C. Prices fall for everyone
D. Nothing changes at all

## 5. Which list correctly names properties of good money?
A. Heavy, rare, shiny, loud
B. Durable, divisible, portable, acceptable, scarce, fungible
C. Fast, slow, hot, cold
D. Edible, colourful, magnetic, secret

---

## Answer key
1. B
2. A
3. B
4. B
5. B
