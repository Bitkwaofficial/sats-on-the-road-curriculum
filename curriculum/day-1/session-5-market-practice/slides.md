# Market practice

Session 5, Day 1
Buy something real in sats

<!-- notes: A field session. Gather the group before heading out. Keep it warm and practical. -->

---

# Today's goal

- Buy a real item from a merchant
- Pay in sats over Lightning
- Handle any problems calmly

<!-- notes: This is the real test of the day. Everyone makes one real payment. -->

---

# The steps

- Agree the price
- Merchant shows a QR code or invoice
- Scan it, check the amount
- Confirm, and both see it arrive

<!-- notes: Walk through once before leaving. Same careful habit: check before you confirm. -->

---

# Be a good guest

- Be patient and polite
- Some merchants are new to this too
- Keep your phone secure
- Thank them

<!-- notes: We are guests in their shop. Do not block other customers. -->

---

# Finding merchants

- Many show a QR code at the till
- BTCMap.org lists shops that accept Bitcoin
- A pilot session was run in Lomé, Togo

<!-- notes: [OWNER TO FILL what is listed near you]. Partners work with a growing merchant network. -->

---

# If there is no signal

- Move to better signal
- Switch wifi and mobile data
- Wait and retry
- Use the stand-in merchant if needed

<!-- notes: USSD options like Machankura exist where there is no smartphone data. [OWNER TO FILL]. -->

---

# If the amount is wrong

- Stop before confirming
- Ask for a fresh invoice
- Never confirm an amount you are unsure of

<!-- notes: Checking the amount is the single most important habit. -->

---

# If a payment fails

- The sats stay with you
- Try again, or ask for a new invoice
- A failed payment is not a lost payment

<!-- notes: Reassure learners. Failures happen and are easy to recover from. -->

---

# The market run

- Go in pairs
- Use your checklist
- Facilitators are here to help

<!-- notes: Keep the queue orderly. Help with scanning, amounts, confirming. -->

# Reflection

- What did you buy, and how did it feel?
- What was smooth? What was tricky?
- Would you use this again? Where?
