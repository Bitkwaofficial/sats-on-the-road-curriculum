# Exercise, Session 5: The market run

**Time:** about 25 minutes in the field, plus a short reflection
**Goal:** make one real purchase from a merchant, paying in sats, and handle any problems calmly.

Go in pairs. Use your funded wallet from Sessions 3 and 4. Tick each box as you go.

## Before you buy
- [ ] My wallet has enough sats for one small item.
- [ ] I have signal or data.
- [ ] I found a merchant who accepts sats (or I am using the stand-in merchant).

## Making the purchase
- [ ] I agreed the price with the merchant in `[local currency]`.
- [ ] The merchant showed me a QR code or invoice.
- [ ] I opened my wallet and tapped "Send."
- [ ] I scanned the QR code or pasted the invoice.
- [ ] I checked the amount matched the agreed price.
- [ ] I confirmed the payment.
- [ ] The merchant saw the sats arrive, and I received my item.

## If something goes wrong
- [ ] No signal: I moved to better signal, switched wifi and data, or used the stand-in merchant.
- [ ] Wrong amount: I stopped and asked for a fresh invoice.
- [ ] Payment failed: I checked the sats were still with me, then tried again.
- [ ] Merchant unsure: I showed my "sent" screen and they refreshed their wallet.

## Keep a simple record
Write down:
- What I bought: ______________________
- Amount in sats: ______________________
- Did it go smoothly? ______________________
- Any problem, and how I fixed it: ______________________

## Reflection (back with the group)
Answer out loud or in writing:
1. How did it feel to pay with sats for something real?
2. What was the trickiest part, and how was it solved?
3. Where in your daily life would you use this again?

## Facilitator checklist
- [ ] A merchant is arranged and ready. [CONFIRM.]
- [ ] Prices are small so everyone can take part. [CONFIRM.]
- [ ] Every wallet is funded. [CONFIRM.]
- [ ] A stand-in merchant is ready in case signal is weak.
- [ ] The queue is kept orderly so the merchant is not overwhelmed.
- [ ] Everyone made at least one real payment before the reflection circle.
