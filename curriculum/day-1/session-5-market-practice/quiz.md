# Quiz, Session 5: Market practice

Five questions. This is for the ambassador badge, not a pass or fail test. Answer key is at the bottom.

## 1. What is the correct order for a real purchase in sats?
A. Confirm first, then agree the price
B. Agree the price, scan the merchant's QR code or invoice, check the amount, confirm
C. Send sats to a random address and hope
D. Pay, then ask what it costs

## 2. Where can you find shops that accept Bitcoin in an area?
A. Only by guessing
B. BTCMap.org, an open map where merchants list themselves
C. The local bank's website
D. Nowhere, there is no way to find them

## 3. The merchant shows an amount that is wrong. What should you do?
A. Confirm it anyway to be polite
B. Stop before confirming and ask for a fresh invoice
C. Send double to be safe
D. Share your seed phrase with the merchant

## 4. Your payment failed. What happened to your sats?
A. They are lost forever
B. They went to the government
C. They stayed with you, and you can try again
D. They were split among other shoppers

## 5. Why is checking the amount before confirming so important?
A. It is not important
B. Because Bitcoin payments cannot be reversed
C. Because the merchant can undo it anyway
D. Because the app charges extra if you do not

---

## Answer key
1. B
2. B
3. B
4. C
5. B
