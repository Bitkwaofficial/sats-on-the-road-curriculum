# Quiz, Session 3: Wallet, seed phrase, and first sats

Five questions. This is for the ambassador badge, not a pass or fail test. Answer key is at the bottom.

## 1. What does a Bitcoin wallet actually hold?
A. The coins themselves, locked inside the app
B. Your keys, which prove the sats on the network are yours
C. A copy of the whole internet
D. Nothing at all

## 2. In a self-custodial wallet, who controls your sats?
A. A company that can freeze them
B. The government
C. You, because you hold your own keys
D. The app store

## 3. What is the correct way to store your seed phrase?
A. Take a photo so you do not lose it
B. Type it into the wallet's website for backup
C. Write it on paper by hand, in order, and keep it safe
D. Send it to yourself in a message

## 4. Someone messages you saying they are support and need your seed phrase to fix a problem. What should you do?
A. Give it to them quickly
B. Never give it; no real support ever needs your seed phrase
C. Give them half of it
D. Post it publicly so others can help

## 5. Before you confirm sending sats, what must you do?
A. Nothing, payments can be undone later
B. Double-check the amount and the recipient, because payments cannot be reversed
C. Turn off your phone
D. Share your seed phrase with the recipient

---

## Answer key
1. B
2. C
3. C
4. B
5. B
