# Exercise, Session 3: Guided wallet setup, receive, and send

**Time:** about 55 minutes of the session, done together with the facilitator
**Goal:** every learner installs a wallet, writes down and protects a seed phrase, receives their first sats, and sends sats to another person.

Work through this with the facilitator. Tick each box as you finish it. Do not skip the seed phrase step.

## Part A: Install and create your wallet
- [ ] I opened the official app store (App Store or Google Play).
- [ ] I searched for `[primary wallet]` and checked the publisher name.
- [ ] I installed the app.
- [ ] I opened the app and chose "Create a new wallet."
- [ ] I set a PIN or passcode for the app, if offered.

Feature-phone users: follow the USSD steps your facilitator gives instead. [CONFIRM: the exact steps for your country.]

## Part B: Write down and protect your seed phrase
This is the most important part. Take your time.
- [ ] My wallet showed me 12 or 24 words.
- [ ] I wrote all the words on paper, by hand, in the exact order, and numbered them.
- [ ] I confirmed the words in the app when it asked.
- [ ] I did NOT take a photo of the words.
- [ ] I did NOT type the words into any website.
- [ ] I did NOT save the words in notes, messages, or email.
- [ ] My paper is folded and kept somewhere safe that only I can reach.

Stop and check: is your seed phrase on paper, correct and in order? Do not move on until it is.

## Part C: Receive your first sats
- [ ] I tapped "Receive" and showed my QR code or address.
- [ ] My facilitator sent me some sats. [CONFIRM amount per learner.]
- [ ] I watched my balance update.

## Part D: Send sats to a partner
Pair up with another learner.
- [ ] My partner showed me their "Receive" QR code or address.
- [ ] I tapped "Send" and scanned the code, or pasted the address or invoice.
- [ ] I entered a small amount in sats.
- [ ] I double-checked the amount and the recipient.
- [ ] I confirmed, and my partner saw the sats arrive.
- [ ] My partner sent some sats back to me the same way.

## Finish line
- [ ] I have a working wallet.
- [ ] My seed phrase is safe on paper.
- [ ] I can receive sats.
- [ ] I can send sats, and I always check before confirming.

## Reflection questions
1. Why must you never photograph your seed phrase or type it into a website?
2. What is the difference between your receive address and your seed phrase?
3. Why can a sent payment not be reversed, and what does that mean for how carefully you should check before confirming?
