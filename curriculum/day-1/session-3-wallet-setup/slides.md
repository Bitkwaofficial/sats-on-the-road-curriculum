# Set up your wallet

Session 3, Day 1
Seed phrase and your first sats

<!-- notes: This is the core of the day. Everyone leaves with a working wallet. Keep pace so no one is left behind. -->

---

# A wallet holds keys, not coins

- Your sats live on the network
- The wallet holds your keys
- Keys prove the sats are yours

<!-- notes: Receive address is like your email. Private key and seed phrase are like your password. -->

---

# Not your keys, not your coins

- Receive address: share it, like an email address
- Private key and seed phrase: never share, like a password
- Whoever holds the keys holds the money

<!-- notes: Repeat this saying through the whole session. -->

---

# Custodial wallet

- A company holds your keys
- Easy to start, they can help you recover
- But they can freeze your money, and if they fail your sats are at risk

<!-- notes: Fine for small amounts and first steps. Like money in someone else's safe. -->

---

# Self-custodial wallet

- You hold your own keys
- No one can freeze or confiscate your sats
- You carry full responsibility

<!-- notes: This is the goal for savings. If you lose your seed phrase, no one can recover it. -->

---

# Real options (neutral)

- Blink: easy start with Lightning
- Phoenix: self-custodial Lightning
- Machankura: Lightning over USSD, no smartphone needed
- Hardware wallets: for savings

# Install and create

- Open the official app store
- Search [primary wallet], check the publisher
- Install, open, choose "Create a new wallet"
- Set a PIN if offered

<!-- notes: Never install from a link someone sent you. Check the maker's name. -->

---

# Your seed phrase (the big one)

- The app shows 12 or 24 words
- This is the master key to all your sats
- Anyone who gets it can take everything

<!-- notes: Slow right down here. This is the most important skill of the day. -->

---

# Write it down, by hand

- On paper, in the exact order, numbered
- Keep it somewhere safe and dry
- Lose phone AND paper, and the sats are gone forever

<!-- notes: Walk the room. Check everyone has actually written it before moving on. -->

---

# Never do these

- Never take a photo of your seed phrase
- Never type it into any website
- Never put it in notes, messages, or email
- No real support will ever ask for it

<!-- notes: Anyone who asks for your seed phrase is trying to rob you. Say this clearly. -->

---

# Receive your first sats

- Tap "Receive"
- Show the QR code or address
- Your address is safe to share
- Watch the balance update

<!-- notes: Send each learner a small amount. [OWNER TO FILL amount and funding source]. -->

---

# Send sats

- Tap "Send"
- Scan the QR code or paste the address
- Enter the amount in sats
- Double-check, then confirm

<!-- notes: Pair learners. Payments cannot be reversed. Always check amount and recipient first. -->

---

# Three things to remember

- You hold your own keys
- Your seed phrase is on paper, never photographed
- Always check before you confirm
