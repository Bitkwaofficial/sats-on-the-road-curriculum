# Quiz, Session 2: What Bitcoin is, in plain words

Five questions. This is for the ambassador badge, not a pass or fail test. Answer key is at the bottom.

## 1. What problem was Bitcoin designed to solve?
A. How to make phones cheaper
B. How to send digital money person to person without needing a bank or central office
C. How to replace the internet
D. How to print more money quickly

## 2. Who is in charge of Bitcoin?
A. A single large bank
B. The person who invented it, who runs it daily
C. No single boss or head office; it is run by the agreement of many people
D. One government

## 3. How many bitcoins will there ever be?
A. As many as needed
B. 21 million, and no more
C. One billion
D. The number changes every year

## 4. How many satoshis are in one bitcoin?
A. 100
B. 1,000
C. 1,000,000
D. 100,000,000

## 5. Which statement is the most honest and balanced?
A. Bitcoin will always make you rich
B. Bitcoin is sound digital money, but it is young and its price can rise and fall
C. Bitcoin can never lose value
D. Bitcoin is controlled by one company you can call for help

---

## Answer key
1. B
2. C
3. B
4. D
5. B
