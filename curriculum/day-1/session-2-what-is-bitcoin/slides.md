# What Bitcoin is, in plain words

Session 2, Day 1
Sats On The Road

<!-- notes: Recap Session 1 in two lines. Fiat loses value because a small group can make more of it. -->

---

# A quick recap

- Money is a shared tool for trade
- Fiat money can be created at will
- When supply grows, each note buys less

<!-- notes: Ask: if you could design money no small group could print more of, what would you want it to do? -->

---

# The problem to solve

- People wanted digital cash, person to person
- Earlier attempts needed a central office
- Shut the office, and the money stops

<!-- notes: If one company runs the money, it can block you or be shut down. -->

---

# A different design

- 2008: a short paper by "Satoshi Nakamoto"
- January 2009: the network started running
- Money with no single company or government in charge

<!-- notes: Satoshi may be a person or a group. The identity is unknown, the goal was clear. -->

---

# Bitcoin is money

- Digital money
- Lives on computers all over the world
- Not in one building

<!-- notes: Most money today is already digital. The difference is no single company controls Bitcoin. -->

---

# Peer-to-peer

- Send it straight to another person
- Like handing over cash
- No bank needs to approve it

<!-- notes: You do not wait for permission. You send directly. -->

---

# No one is in charge

- No boss, no head office
- No one can switch it off
- No one can change the rules for themselves

<!-- notes: This is its strength. It also means no hotline to call, which we address in Session 3. -->

---

# An agreement among people

- Like a board game with shared rules
- Everyone follows the same rules
- Cheat, and the others reject you

<!-- notes: If a player tries to pay with the wrong thing, others simply stop playing with them. The game continues. -->

---

# A fixed limit: 21 million

- There will only ever be 21 million bitcoins
- No one can make extra for themselves
- The network rejects any attempt

<!-- notes: Compare to fiat, which can be created at will. Fixed supply means no small group can dilute it. -->

---

# Sats: the everyday unit

- 1 bitcoin = 100,000,000 satoshis
- We call them "sats"
- You do not need a whole bitcoin to use it

<!-- notes: Most people deal in sats day to day. A coffee is a few hundred or thousand sats. -->

---

# Sound digital money

- Scarce, with a fixed limit
- Hard to counterfeit
- Open to anyone with a phone and internet

<!-- notes: This is what "sound digital money" means. Keep it plain. -->

---

# An honest note

- Bitcoin is young, like the internet in the 1990s
- Its price in [local currency] can rise and fall
- A tool to learn and use, not a promise of profit

<!-- notes: No financial advice, no predictions. Be balanced. -->

---
