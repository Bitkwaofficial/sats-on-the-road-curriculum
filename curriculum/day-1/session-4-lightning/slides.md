# Lightning ⚡

Session 4, Day 1
Paying and getting paid

<!-- notes: Recap Session 3. Everyone has a wallet and has sent and received sats. -->

---

# The problem

- On-chain payments are very secure
- But they take about ten minutes
- And can cost more in fees

<!-- notes: For a coffee or a taxi fare, we want faster and cheaper. -->

---

# What Lightning is

- A fast layer built on top of Bitcoin
- A quick lane for small payments
- Instant, and very cheap

<!-- notes: Bitcoin is the strong base. Lightning is the fast layer on top. Same sats. -->

---

# The café tab

- Open a tab for the day
- Pay as you go
- Settle the final total at the end

<!-- notes: Lightning works in a similar way. Payments are instant and cheap. -->

---

# On-chain vs Lightning

- On-chain: very secure, about 10 minutes, best for larger amounts
- Lightning: instant, very low fees, best for everyday spending

<!-- notes: Use the right tool for the job. Large savings: on-chain. Daily spend: Lightning. -->

---

# A Lightning invoice

- A request for a payment
- The receiver creates it, often a QR code
- Can include a set amount
- Used up once it is paid

<!-- notes: To get paid: tap Receive, choose Lightning, enter amount, show the QR code. -->

---

# To pay an invoice

- Tap "Send"
- Scan the invoice or QR code
- Check the amount
- Confirm

<!-- notes: Same careful habit as Session 3. Check before you confirm. -->

---

# A Lightning address

- Looks like an email address
- name@example.com
- Reusable: people can pay you again and again

- The Sats On The Road fundraiser receives Lightning
- Address: satsontheroadafrica@geyser.fund

# Live demonstration

- Sats relay across the room
- Then pay the facilitator
- Watch it arrive in seconds

# When to use which

- Lightning: small daily payments
- On-chain: larger amounts and savings
- Keep only spending money on your phone

<!-- notes: Repeat Session 3 safety. Seed phrase on paper, never photographed. -->

---

# An honest note

- Payments can sometimes fail; the sats stay with the sender
- Just try again
- No financial advice, no predictions

<!-- notes: Balanced and calm. Lightning is young but works well for daily spending. -->
