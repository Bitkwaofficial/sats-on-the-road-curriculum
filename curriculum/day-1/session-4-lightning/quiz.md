# Quiz, Session 4: Lightning, paying and getting paid

Five questions. This is for the ambassador badge, not a pass or fail test. Answer key is at the bottom.

## 1. What is the Lightning Network?
A. A different money that is not Bitcoin
B. A fast layer built on top of Bitcoin for quick, cheap payments
C. A bank that holds your Bitcoin
D. A way to print more bitcoins

## 2. Compared with an on-chain payment, a Lightning payment is usually:
A. Slower and more expensive
B. Faster and cheaper, best for small everyday payments
C. Exactly the same in every way
D. Only for very large amounts

## 3. What is a Lightning invoice?
A. A tax bill from the government
B. A request for a payment that the receiver creates, often as a QR code
C. Your seed phrase
D. A receipt you get after a bank transfer

## 4. How is a Lightning address different from an invoice?
A. There is no difference
B. An address is reusable, like an email address, while an invoice is usually a one-time request
C. An address can only be used once
D. An invoice never expires

## 5. If a Lightning payment fails, what happens to the sats?
A. They disappear forever
B. They go to the government
C. They stay with the sender, who can try again
D. They are split between strangers

---

## Answer key
1. B
2. B
3. B
4. B
5. C
