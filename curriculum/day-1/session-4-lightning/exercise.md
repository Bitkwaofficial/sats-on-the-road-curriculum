# Exercise, Session 4: Lightning live demonstration

**Time:** about 14 minutes
**Goal:** see and feel how fast and cheap Lightning is by passing sats across the room and paying a live invoice.

Everyone uses the wallet they set up in Session 3. Make sure each wallet has a small amount of sats to practice with. [OWNER TO FILL: amount.]

## Part 1: The sats relay (about 8 min)
Split the room into groups of 3 to 5, standing or sitting in a line (person A, B, C, and so on).

1. The facilitator sends a starting amount of sats to person A in each group.
2. Person A creates a Lightning invoice or shows their receive QR code, then person A sends the sats on to person B.
   - To send: tap "Send," scan person B's invoice or QR code, enter the amount, check it, confirm.
3. Person B passes the sats to person C the same way, and so on down the line.
4. The goal is to get the sats from the first person to the last person in the group.
5. The first group to reach the last person wins, and keeps the sats.

Facilitator tips:
- Keep amounts small.
- Walk the line and help anyone who is stuck creating or scanning an invoice.
- Point out how fast each hop is, usually just a few seconds.

## Part 2: Pay a live invoice (about 4 min)
1. The facilitator shows one invoice or Lightning address on a screen or large phone, for a small amount.
2. Two or three volunteers each pay it from their wallet, one at a time.
3. The room watches the payment arrive in seconds.
4. Point out the tiny fee compared to the speed.

## Part 3: Quick debrief (about 2 min)
Ask the group:
- "Was sending a payment easy?"
- "Was it fast? Was it cheap?"
- "Where in your daily life could you use this: transport, food, airtime, market stalls?"

Draw out the point: Lightning makes small, everyday Bitcoin payments practical. This is exactly what you will do with a real merchant in Session 5.

## Reflection questions for learners
1. What is the difference between a Lightning invoice and a Lightning address?
2. Why is Lightning better than on-chain for buying a coffee, and when would you still use on-chain?
3. What happens to the sats if a Lightning payment fails?
