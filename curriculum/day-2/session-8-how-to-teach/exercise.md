# Exercise, Practice-teach a five-minute segment

**Time:** about 20 minutes
**Work in:** pairs
**You need:** the Day 1 session plans and handouts, a timer, and the slips of common questions and myths.

This is the heart of the session. You will teach a short piece of the course to one person, as if they have never seen Bitcoin before. Teaching one unsure beginner is the real skill, and it is easier than standing in front of a crowd.

## Set up (2 min)

- Find a partner. Decide who teaches first.
- The teacher picks one five-minute segment to deliver, for example:
  - What money is and why ours loses value
  - What Bitcoin is, in plain words
  - How to write down and protect a seed phrase
  - How to receive one Lightning payment
- The learner takes one or two question slips to ask during the teach.

## Round 1 (7 min)

- **Teach (5 min).** Teach your segment. Use plain words. Get your partner to do something, even if it is just tapping through a wallet screen or repeating a phrase back.
- **Learner's job.** Play an interested but unsure beginner. Ask your slip questions at a natural moment. Do not try to trip the teacher up; just be a real beginner.
- **Timekeeper.** Call time at five minutes so the teacher practises finishing on time.

## Feedback (2 min)

The learner gives exactly two notes:
- One thing that worked well and should be kept.
- One specific thing to try next time.

Keep it kind and concrete. "You explained the seed phrase clearly" is better than "good job".

## Round 2 (7 min)

Swap roles and repeat. New teacher, new segment, new feedback.

## Debrief (2 min)

As a pair, agree on one habit you both want to carry into your real sessions.

## Facilitator notes

- Circulate and listen. Note one strong moment from each pair to name in the whole-group debrief.
- If a teacher freezes, prompt gently: "What is the one thing you want them to remember?"
- Remind everyone that finishing on time is itself a skill worth practising.
- If you have an odd number, make one group of three with two learners.
