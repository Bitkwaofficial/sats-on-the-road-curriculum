# Quiz, How to teach this

Five questions for the ambassador badge. This is not a pass-or-fail test. It is a way to check that the ideas landed.

---

**1. How do adults learn best?**

- A) By listening to a long, detailed lecture
- B) By doing something themselves, then understanding it
- C) By memorising technical terms first
- D) By being told they are wrong until they get it right

**2. A community session succeeds when people leave having:**

- A) Heard a lot of information
- B) Taken careful notes
- C) Done something themselves, like making a real payment
- D) Agreed with everything the teacher said

**3. A learner asks a question you cannot answer. What is the best response?**

- A) Make up an answer that sounds confident
- B) Change the subject quickly
- C) Say you don't know, write it down, and find out together
- D) Tell them the question is not important

**4. Which is the most common mistake for a new facilitator?**

- A) Finishing early
- B) Talking too long and running out of time for the hands-on part
- C) Using too many local examples
- D) Letting people use their own phones

**5. Which technique keeps a room engaged?**

- A) Reading every slide word for word
- B) Standing in one place the whole time
- C) Asking questions, pairing people up, and using local examples
- D) Using as much technical vocabulary as possible

---

## Answer key

1. **B**, Adults learn by doing first, understanding second.
2. **C**, Doing, not just hearing, is the measure of a good session.
3. **C**, Honesty builds more trust than a confident wrong answer.
4. **B**, Talking too long and skipping the hands-on part is the classic error.
5. **C**, Questions, pairs, movement, and local examples keep people with you.
