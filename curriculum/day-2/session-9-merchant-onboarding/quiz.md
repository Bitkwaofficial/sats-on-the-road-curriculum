# Quiz, Onboarding a merchant

Five questions for the ambassador badge. This is not a pass-or-fail test. It is a way to check that the ideas landed.

---

**1. What is the strongest way to convince a merchant during onboarding?**

- A) Explain how the blockchain works in detail
- B) Do a small real test payment and let them watch it land
- C) Promise the price will go up
- D) Tell them everyone else is already doing it

**2. During setup, who should write down and keep the merchant's seed phrase?**

- A) The ambassador, to keep it safe
- B) The merchant, who keeps it private
- C) The country lead
- D) No one; it is not needed

**3. A merchant worries about the price falling. What is the honest answer?**

- A) "The price only goes up"
- B) "You can cash out to local currency the same day if you prefer"
- C) "Do not worry about it"
- D) "You must hold it for a year"

**4. How does a merchant confirm a payment arrived, after you have gone?**

- A) They call you every time
- B) They check the confirmed payment in their wallet history
- C) They wait for a bank statement
- D) They cannot check it themselves

**5. What does it mean for a merchant to be fully onboarded?**

- A) They have heard the pitch
- B) They have installed an app
- C) The sign is up, a real payment has landed, and they can check a sale on their own
- D) They have promised to try it later

---

## Answer key

1. **B**, Seeing a real payment land does the convincing.
2. **B**, Never handle a merchant's seed phrase; they write and keep it.
3. **B**, Same-day cash-out is the honest answer; never promise a price direction.
4. **B**, The wallet history shows confirmed payments.
5. **C**, Signage up, a real payment made, and able to check sales alone.
