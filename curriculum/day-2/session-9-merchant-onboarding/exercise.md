# Exercise, Role-play a merchant onboarding

**Time:** about 13 minutes
**Work in:** pairs
**You need:** two phones with the teaching wallet, a small amount of sats on the "customer" phone, a printed [merchant onboarding checklist](../../../merchant-kit/merchant-onboarding-checklist.md), and a "Bitcoin accepted here" sign from the merchant kit.

You will practise the whole onboarding from start to finish, working down the checklist, so that the real thing at a market stall feels familiar.

## Set up (1 min)

- Decide who is the **ambassador** and who is the **merchant** first.
- The merchant should play someone busy and a little unsure, for example a food seller or a phone-credit vendor who has heard of Bitcoin but never used it.
- Put the printed checklist between you.

## Round 1 (5 min)

Work straight down the checklist:

1. **Pitch the value** in under a minute.
2. **Set up receiving** on the merchant phone (find the Lightning address or Receive QR; talk through the seed-phrase backup without touching it).
3. **Do a test payment** from the customer phone and watch it land on the merchant screen.
4. **Confirm it arrived** by finding it in the wallet history together.
5. **Show cash-out** by naming the options that work here and what the merchant would do.
6. **Leave the signage** by placing the sign and agreeing a follow-up.

The merchant should raise at least one worry during the role-play, for example "will I lose money if the price drops?"

## Feedback (1 min)

The merchant gives two notes: one thing that felt reassuring, one thing that would have lost them in real life.

## Round 2 (5 min)

Swap roles and run the full checklist again.

## Debrief (1 min)

Agree the one step you each want to slow down and do better at a real stall.

## Facilitator notes

- Insist on a real test payment, not a pretend one. Seeing the sats land is the whole point.
- Watch for ambassadors who talk too long before touching a phone. Nudge them to set up receiving sooner.
- Make sure no one handles the "merchant's" seed phrase for them, even in play.
- Reset wallets and recover any test sats afterwards.
