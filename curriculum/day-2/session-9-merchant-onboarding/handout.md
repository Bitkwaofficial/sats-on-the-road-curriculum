# Onboarding a merchant, a one-page guide

Keep this with you at the stall. Be quick, be honest, and leave the merchant able to do it without you. For the full detail, see the [merchant guide](../../../merchant-kit/merchant-guide.md) and work from the [merchant onboarding checklist](../../../merchant-kit/merchant-onboarding-checklist.md).

## The six steps

1. **Explain the value.** Get paid directly, low fees on Lightning, no chargebacks once confirmed, cash out to `[local currency]` when you want.
2. **Set up receiving.** Install the wallet from the official source, help them create and back up a seed phrase (they keep it), and find the Lightning address or Receive QR.
3. **Do a test payment.** Send a small real payment and let them watch it land on their own screen.
4. **Check it arrived.** Show them the wallet history so they can confirm any sale later without you.
5. **Show cash-out.** Walk the options that actually work here: `[OWNER TO FILL per country]`. Never promise a rate.
6. **Leave the signage.** Place the "Bitcoin accepted here" sign and QR stand, record the merchant, and agree a follow-up.

## The one-minute pitch

> "You can accept Bitcoin here. Payments come straight to you, the fees are tiny, and once it is confirmed it cannot be reversed. You can change it to `[local currency]` the same day if you like. Setup takes a few minutes and I will do it with you now."

## Rules that keep merchants

- **Respect their time.** If they are serving a customer, wait or come back.
- **Show, do not just tell.** The test payment does the convincing.
- **Never touch their seed phrase.** Guide them; they write it and keep it.
- **Be honest about price.** The price can move, which is why we show same-day cash-out.
- **Follow up.** Return or message to check their first customer sales and answer questions.

- *"Will I lose money if the price drops?"* You can cash out the same day; you hold Bitcoin only as long as you choose.
