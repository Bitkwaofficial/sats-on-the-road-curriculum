# Onboarding a merchant

Step by step, in the stall

Session 9 · Day 2 · 60 min

<!-- notes: Every onboarded merchant gives dozens of learners a place to spend. Respect the merchant's time above all. -->

---

# Why merchants matter

No merchants, no circular economy.

A learner who can spend needs someone who can receive.

<!-- notes: Set the tone: quick, respectful, never pushy. -->

---

# The value, in under a minute

- Get paid directly, no chargebacks once confirmed
- Low fees on Lightning ⚡
- Reach customers who hold Bitcoin
- Cash out to [local currency] when you want

<!-- notes: Sell the problem you solve, not the technology. Practise saying it in one breath. -->

---

# The flow

1. Explain the value
2. Set up receiving
3. Do a test payment
4. Check it arrived
5. Show cash-out
6. Leave the signage

<!-- notes: This is the whole session on one slide. Everything else fills it in. -->

---

# Set up receiving

- Install from the official source only
- Create and back up a seed phrase (they keep it)
- Find the Lightning address or Receive QR
- Show the QR on the phone or a printed stand

<!-- notes: Never touch their seed phrase. Be clear about custodial vs self-custody. -->

---

# Do a test payment

Send a small, real payment.

Let the merchant watch their own screen.

The moment it lands is the moment they believe.

<!-- notes: Talk less, show the sats landing. Then read the amount in [local currency]. -->

---

# Check it arrived

Show them the wallet history.

A confirmed payment shows there.

Now they can check any sale without you.

<!-- notes: This is what lets them run it alone after you leave. -->

---

# Show cash-out, honestly

Options where they work locally:

[CONFIRM per country: e.g. Vexl, Banxaas, BitSpenda, Machankura]

Never promise a rate.

<!-- notes: Only name options that actually work in this country. Walk one end to end if time allows. -->

---

# Leave the signage

Place the "Bitcoin accepted here" sign and QR stand.

Add the merchant to the stop's record.

Agree a simple follow-up.

<!-- notes: See the merchant kit for printable materials. -->

---

# Role-play

One plays a busy merchant.

One plays the ambassador.

Work straight down the checklist, then swap.

# Remember

- Respect their time
- Show, do not just tell
- Never promise a price
- Leave them able to do it alone
