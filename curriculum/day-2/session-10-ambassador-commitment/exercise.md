# Exercise, Fill in your first monthly report and plan

**Time:** about 10 minutes (fits inside the session, or take home)
**Work:** on your own, then share in pairs
**You need:** the monthly report template from the [ambassador playbook](../../../playbook/ambassador-playbook.md), and a pen.

Before you commit, practise the thing you will actually do every month. A report you have filled in once is far less daunting the second time.

## Part 1, A practice report (5 min)

Fill in the monthly report template as if one month has already passed. Make honest, realistic numbers for your own town, not big dreams:

- Sessions you ran, and roughly how many people came.
- People you helped onboard to a wallet.
- Merchants you onboarded.
- One thing that worked well.
- One thing that was hard.
- One thing you need help with.

## Part 2, Your first-month plan (3 min)

Write three lines:

1. The date you will run your first session.
2. One merchant you already have in mind to approach.
3. One person who will help or co-teach with you.

## Part 3, Share (2 min)

Tell your partner your first-month plan in two sentences. Ask them for one idea or one contact that could help.

## Facilitator notes

- This doubles as a gentle readiness check. Someone who cannot name a single realistic next step may not be ready to commit yet, and that is fine.
- Remind them the real report is due `[OWNER TO FILL: monthly report due date]` and is discussed on the monthly call.
- Collect no personal data here; the practice report stays with the ambassador.
