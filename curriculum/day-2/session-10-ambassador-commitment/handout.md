# What next

The two days are over, but this is a beginning. Bitcoin in Africa is still early, and the people who start building now become the leaders of this ecosystem later. You do not need a special degree, a visa, wealth, or anyone's permission. You need skills, consistency, and proof of work: showing what you can do, not just saying it.

## Paths you can take

- **Education and community.** Teach, translate, organise meetups, guide onboarding. If you can explain simply, you can lead.
- **Entrepreneurship.** Build Bitcoin-first products or services, and onboard merchants in your town.
- **Creative and media.** Tell stories, design, make short explainer videos in your own language.
- **Product and operations.** Keep projects organised and moving.
- **Technical.** Learn to build wallets and Lightning tools, and contribute to open-source.

## Show your proof of work

Pick one path, pick one thing to make, and share it publicly:

- Onboard one merchant and show Lightning payments working.
- Make a simple Bitcoin explainer in your language.
- Run a small onboarding event.
- Contribute a fix to an open-source project.

This is how people find you.
