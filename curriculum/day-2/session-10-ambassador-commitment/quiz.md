# Quiz, The ambassador commitment

Five questions for the ambassador badge. This is not a pass-or-fail test. It is a way to check that the ideas landed.

---

**1. What is an ambassador's main role after the trip moves on?**

- A) To sell Bitcoin for profit
- B) To keep teaching, onboarding merchants, and supporting the community
- C) To collect the stipend and nothing more
- D) To manage the vehicle

**2. How is the stipend best described?**

- A) A guaranteed salary paid no matter what
- B) A support for costs that follows real work and reporting
- C) A reward for predicting the price
- D) A one-time joining bonus

**3. Which of these would get an ambassador removed?**

- A) Missing a target and reporting it honestly
- B) Asking a learner to hand over their seed phrase
- C) Running a small session
- D) Asking for help on the monthly call

**4. What is the monthly report for?**

- A) To test your spelling
- B) To share what you did, what was hard, and what you need, so the network can learn and support you
- C) To compete with other ambassadors
- D) It is optional and rarely used

**5. You are not sure you are ready to commit today. What is the right thing to do?**

- A) Sign anyway to avoid awkwardness
- B) Never speak to the team again
- C) Say "not yet", keep teaching informally, and commit when you are ready
- D) Demand the stipend first

---

## Answer key

1. **B**, The role is to keep the work going for the community.
2. **B**, The stipend follows the work; it is support, not a salary.
3. **B**, Taking a seed phrase is a serious breach of the code of conduct.
4. **B**, The report helps the network learn and send support.
5. **C**, A thoughtful "not yet" is welcome and honourable.
