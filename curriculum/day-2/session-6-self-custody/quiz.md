# Quiz, Self-custody deeper

Five questions for the ambassador badge. This is not a pass-or-fail test. It is a way to check that the ideas landed.

---

**1. What does "not your keys, not your coins" mean?**

- A) You must buy a hardware wallet before using Bitcoin
- B) If someone else holds the private keys, you do not truly control the coins
- C) Keys are only needed for large amounts
- D) You should share your keys with support staff

**2. Your seed phrase is best described as:**

- A) A password you can reset at any time
- B) A backup of your transaction history
- C) The words that rebuild your wallet and control your coins
- D) A code the wallet company keeps for you

**3. Which of these is a safe way to store a seed phrase?**

- A) A screenshot saved to the cloud
- B) A message to yourself in a chat app
- C) Written by hand on card, kept private, with a second copy elsewhere
- D) Typed into a website that offers to keep it safe

**4. Why do we test a recovery?**

- A) To earn more sats
- B) To confirm the backup actually works before we rely on it
- C) Because the wallet stops working if you do not
- D) To delete the old wallet

**5. Someone messages you: "Send me 10,000 sats and I will send you 20,000 back." What is this?**

- A) A normal Lightning feature
- B) A reward for new users
- C) A scam. Don't trust, verify
- D) A node asking for a fee

---

## Answer key

1. **B**, Control of the keys is control of the coins.
2. **C**, The seed phrase rebuilds the private keys; whoever has it controls the coins.
3. **C**, Offline, private, and with a second copy in a different place.
4. **B**, An untested backup is only a guess.
5. **C**, "Send first, get more back" is always a scam.
