# Self-custody, deeper

Backups, recovery, and what not to do

Session 6 · Day 2 · 60 min

<!-- notes: This is the ambassador day. These people will soon be the only Bitcoin help in their town. Keep the tone steady, not scary. -->

---

# Not your keys, not your coins

If you do not hold the keys, you do not truly own the coins.

Someone else can freeze, lose, or block them.

<!-- notes: The core saying from Chapter 3. Say it, write it, repeat it. -->

---

# Custodial vs self-custody

| | Custodial | Self-custody |
|---|---|---|
| Who holds the keys | The provider | You |
| Reset button | Yes | No |
| Can be frozen | Yes | No |
| If you lose the backup | They can help | Coins are gone |

Honest message: small and short-term can be custodial. Savings should be self-custody.

<!-- notes: Both halves of the deal. Do not oversell self-custody as risk-free. -->

---

# Your seed phrase IS your wallet

12 or 24 words, in order.

Anyone with the words controls the coins.

Lose the words with no backup, and the coins are gone forever.

<!-- notes: The coins are not in the phone. The phrase is the proof of ownership. -->

---

# Safe ways to back up

- Write it by hand, in order, on paper or card
- Keep a second copy in a different safe place
- For savings, a metal backup survives fire and water

<!-- notes: Have each person hand-write a practice phrase now. -->

---

# Never safe

- A screenshot or photo
- The cloud, email, or a note app that syncs
- A message to yourself in any chat app
- Reading the words aloud to anyone

<!-- notes: Convenience is the enemy of key safety. -->

---

# Test your recovery

A backup you have never tested is a guess.

Restore the practice wallet on a second phone.

Watch the sats come back.

<!-- notes: This is the exercise. The coins live on the network, not the phone. -->

---

# How people lose their coins

- Screenshots and cloud backups
- Sharing the seed phrase with "support"
- Typing the phrase into a website or pop-up
- Fake-wallet apps that steal the phrase

<!-- notes: Ask ambassadors to add scams they have seen locally. -->

---

# No real support ever needs your seed phrase

Anyone who asks for it is stealing from you.

As an ambassador, never ask a learner to show theirs.

<!-- notes: Model the behaviour you want them to teach. -->

---

# Scams to name out loud

- "Send 1, get 2 back"
- Giveaways and airdrops that ask you to send first
- "The next Bitcoin" and quick-profit promises

Don't trust, verify.

<!-- notes: Chapter 3 mantra. Applies to every claim. -->

---

# Cold storage for savings

Separate spending from savings.

- Mobile wallet: daily spending
- Hardware (cold) wallet: long-term savings, keys offline

Same rules: back up the seed, test the recovery.

<!-- notes: [CONFIRM: whether a specific hardware wallet is recommended per country] -->

---

# Remember

- The coins live on the network, not in the phone
- The seed phrase is the only proof they are yours
- A backup you have not tested is not a backup
