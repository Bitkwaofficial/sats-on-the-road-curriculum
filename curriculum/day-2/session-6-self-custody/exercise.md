# Exercise, Test a recovery

**Time:** about 13 minutes
**Work in:** pairs
**You need:** two phones per pair (or take turns on one), the teaching wallet installed, a "practice" wallet that already holds a tiny amount of sats, and its seed phrase written on a card.

The goal is to feel, with your own hands, that the coins live on the network and not inside one phone. If the phone breaks, a good backup brings the coins back.

## Steps

1. **Look at the practice wallet.** On the first phone, open the practice wallet and note the small balance. This is the wallet you will "lose" and recover.

2. **Prepare the backup.** Confirm you have the practice wallet's seed phrase written on the card, in order. Do not photograph it.

3. **Simulate a lost phone.** On the second phone, install the same wallet app from the official source only. Choose **Restore** or **Import an existing wallet**, then **Restore with recovery phrase**.

4. **Enter the seed phrase.** Type the words one by one, in the correct order, from the card. Take your time.

5. **Confirm recovery.** Finish the restore. Wait for the wallet to sync, then check that the same small balance appears. The coins came back from the network, not from the old phone.

6. **Swap roles.** The other person in the pair repeats steps 1 to 5.

## Talk about it after

- How did it feel to see the balance return on a different phone?
- What would have happened if the card had been lost, or photographed and the phone hacked?
- Where will you keep your own seed phrase after today, and where will you keep a second copy?

## Facilitator notes

- Use a wallet with only a tiny, replaceable amount. Never use anyone's real savings for a drill.
- Reset the practice wallets on the borrowed phones afterwards so no sats are left behind.
- If you only have one phone per pair, back up and then restore on the same phone instead. The lesson still lands.
- Watch for the most common slip: words entered out of order. Patience fixes it.
