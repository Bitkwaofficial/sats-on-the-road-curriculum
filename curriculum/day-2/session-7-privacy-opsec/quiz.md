# Quiz, Privacy and opsec

Five questions for the ambassador badge. Not pass or fail. Discuss the answers
together.

**1. Bitcoin is best described as:**
- a) Completely anonymous, no one can ever trace it
- b) Pseudonymous: public and permanent, with no name attached but traceable
- c) Private by default, nothing is recorded
- d) Only visible to the government

**2. Someone messages an activist: "I can help recover your lost funds, just send
me your seed phrase." What is this?**
- a) A normal support process
- b) Fine, if they work for the wallet company
- c) A scam, always
- d) Acceptable if the amount is small

**3. Which habit most reduces the chance of linking your payments together?**
- a) Posting your balance so supporters trust you
- b) Reusing one donation address for a whole year
- c) Using a new address for each payment, and Lightning for small ones
- d) Sharing a screenshot of each payment

**4. Why use multisig for a group's funds?**
- a) It makes payments faster
- b) So one seized phone or one arrest cannot move the money alone
- c) So the government can audit it
- d) It removes all fees

**5. What is the honest boundary of this training?**
- a) It helps people hide crimes
- b) It helps evade all taxes
- c) It helps ordinary people organise, report and survive safely, within the law
- d) It guarantees no one can ever be caught

---

### Answer key
1: b · 2: c · 3: c · 4: b · 5: c
