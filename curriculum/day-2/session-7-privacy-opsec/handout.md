# Handout, Privacy and opsec (one page)

The full version is the
[opsec handout for activists and journalists](../../../safety/opsec-for-activists-and-journalists.md).
This page is the short reminder to keep in your pocket.

## Remember one thing

**Bitcoin is pseudonymous, not anonymous.** The blockchain is public and
permanent. Your name is not on it, but careless habits can reveal you. Privacy is
practised, not assumed.

## Seven habits

1. Never reuse an address (let the wallet make a new one each time).
2. Never share a screenshot, your balance, or your xpub.
3. Keep separate wallets: donations, spending, savings.
4. Use Lightning for small and frequent payments.
5. Protect the phone: lock, PIN, updates, a clean device for sensitive work.
6. Public fundraising: pseudonym plus a Lightning address, never a live balance.
7. Custody: paper or metal seed, two safe places, multisig for group money.

## The golden rule

**If anyone asks for your seed phrase or private key, it is a scam.** Every time.

## Watch for

- Fake wallet links and "restore your wallet" messages.
- "Double your Bitcoin" and guaranteed returns.
- One phone holding a whole group's funds.

## If a device is seized or someone is detained

- Group funds use multisig, so one phone cannot move them.
- Follow the [team safety protocol](../../../safety/team-safety-protocol.md).
- Contact your country lead and legal aid. Plan this before you need it.

## The boundary

This is not about breaking the law or hiding a crime. It is about keeping the
ability to organise, report and survive when money is used against people. Check
what is legal where you are.
