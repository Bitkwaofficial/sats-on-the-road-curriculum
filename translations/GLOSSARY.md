# Glossary, 40 Bitcoin terms

This is the shared vocabulary for the whole curriculum. **Translate this first.**
Agreeing one word per term, per language, keeps every handout, slide and session
consistent. If a term has no good local word yet, keep the English or French word
and add a short explanation in the local language.

- **French (`fr`)** is filled in as a starting point; improve it if a better local
  French usage exists.
- **Twi (`tw`), Fon (`fon`), Ewe (`ewe`), Mooré (`mos`)** are left blank `[ ]` for
  the country translators to agree. Fill them in and open a pull request.

## How to add a language

1. Copy the `English` and `Plain meaning` columns.
2. Add your column (use the ISO code, e.g. `ha` for Hausa).
3. Agree one word per term with your co-translators before translating handouts.
4. Open a pull request, or an issue titled "Offer a translation".
