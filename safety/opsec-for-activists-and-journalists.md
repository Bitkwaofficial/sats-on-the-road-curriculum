# Opsec for activists and journalists

For people who may be targeted for what they say or who they support. Bitcoin can let you receive support that no bank can freeze, but only if you use it with care. Keep this to one page and practise these habits.

## Hold your own keys

Use a self-custodial wallet, one where only you hold the keys. A custodial app or exchange can freeze your funds, hand over your records, or be shut down. A self-custodial wallet cannot be frozen and answers to no one but you.

## Keep your identities separate

Bitcoin is pseudonymous, not anonymous: every on-chain payment is public and permanent. Keep one wallet for public donations and a separate wallet for your savings, and never move funds straight between them. The donation wallet is public; the savings wallet stays private.

## Never reuse an on-chain address

Use a new address for every payment (most modern wallets do this for you). A reused address links your payments together and can tie them back to you.

## Use Lightning for small support

Receive small, frequent support over Lightning. Lightning payments are not written to the public blockchain one by one, so they leak far less, and they are faster and cheaper. Publish a Lightning address rather than a fixed on-chain address.

## Back up your seed phrase offline

Write your seed phrase on paper or metal and keep a copy in two safe places. Never type it into a website, never save it to the cloud, and never take a photo of it on your phone. Anyone who asks for your seed phrase is trying to steal from you.

## If a device is seized

Keep your phone locked with a strong PIN before you ever need to. If a phone is taken, move any funds from a safe device using your backup, and tell the others in your group. For group funds, use multisig so one seized phone cannot drain the fund. See the [team safety protocol](./team-safety-protocol.md).

## Who not to tell

Do not tell anyone how much you hold, where your savings wallet is, or where your seed backups are kept. Tell only one trusted person who could recover the funds if you cannot. The fewer who know, the safer you are. ₿
