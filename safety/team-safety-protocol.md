# Team safety protocol

For the Sats On The Road crew and local ambassadors on the ground. Read it before every leg. Fill in the country-specific blanks with your country lead.

## Checkpoints

- Be calm, polite and brief. You teach people to use open-source money, so a simple honest answer is best: financial education.
- The driver carries the vehicle papers, the rental agreement and insurance, their own licence and ID, and a copy of any filming permission letter.
- Each team member carries their passport or ID and, where needed, the permission letters for filming in markets and schools.
- Do not argue, do not film, and do not hand over passwords or PINs unless legally compelled. Know your local rights in advance.

## Filming in markets

- Ask before you film anyone. Use the [consent form](./consent-form.md) or the verbal consent script for people who do not read.
- Offer a face-blur option and honour it. Some people cannot be shown safely.
- Do not show the faces of uniformed officers (police, soldiers, customs).
- Do not film at borders or airports.
- Do not film anyone's screen showing a seed phrase, address or balance.
- If someone asks to stop, or to be removed later, stop, and remove it.

## If a device is seized

- Keep every phone locked with a strong PIN at all times.
- Back up footage nightly to two drives. One drive stays at the hotel and one travels with the team, so a seized or lost device never holds the only copy.
- Group funds use multisig, so a single seized phone cannot move them. Confirm the other key-holders are safe and the fund is intact.
- If a personal phone with a hot wallet is taken, move those funds from a safe device using the backup.
- Record what happened, where, and who was involved, once you are safe. Contact local legal aid or a press-freedom organisation if anyone is detained.
