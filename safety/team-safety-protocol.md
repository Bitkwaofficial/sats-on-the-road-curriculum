# Team safety protocol

For the Sats On The Road crew and local ambassadors on the ground. Read it before
every leg. Fill in the country-specific blanks with your country lead.

## Before you travel to a town

- [ ] Confirm the local country lead and a backup contact.
- [ ] Agree the venue, and a fallback venue.
- [ ] Check whether any permit is needed to teach or film in the market or school,
      and get it. `[CONFIRM: local permit rules]`
- [ ] Save offline maps and the route; agree a meeting point if the group splits.
- [ ] Charge power banks; carry a backup phone.
- [ ] Agree who carries the float of sats and how much (keep it small).

## Money safety

- Carry only a small amount of sats for demonstrations on a phone wallet.
- Keep the main float and any donations in a wallet **no single person on the
  ground controls alone** (use multisig, see
  [opsec](./opsec-for-activists-and-journalists.md)).
- Never show large balances on screen or on camera.
- Do not announce in advance, publicly, where and when cash or devices will be.

## Filming in markets and public places

- Ask before you film anyone. Use the [consent form](./consent-form.md) or the
  verbal consent script for people who do not read.
- Offer a **face-blur** option and honour it. Some people cannot be shown safely.
- Do not film police, soldiers, checkpoints or government buildings.
- Do not film anyone's screen showing a seed phrase, address or balance.
- If someone asks to stop or to be removed later, stop, and remove it.

## At checkpoints and with authorities

- Be calm, polite and brief. You are teaching people to use open-source money.
- Keep documents ready. `[CONFIRM: which documents per country]`
- Do not argue. Do not film. Do not hand over passwords or PINs unless legally
  compelled; know your local rights in advance. `[CONFIRM: local legal rights]`
- If asked what you do, a simple honest answer is best: financial education.

## If a device is seized or someone is detained

1. Stay calm and account for everyone in the group.
2. Contact the country lead and the emergency contact immediately (below).
3. Because the group's funds use multisig, a single seized phone cannot move
   them. Confirm the other key-holders are safe and the fund is intact.
4. If a personal phone with a hot wallet is taken, move those funds from a safe
   device using the backup, if any balance remains.
5. Record what happened, where, and who was involved, once you are safe.
6. Contact a local legal aid or press-freedom organisation if a detention occurs.
   `[CONFIRM: local legal aid / press-freedom contacts per country]`
