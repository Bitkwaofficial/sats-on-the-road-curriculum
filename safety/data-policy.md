# Data policy

Sats On The Road works with people who can be put at risk by what they do:
activists, journalists, and the communities around them. This page says, in plain
words, what we collect, what we publish, what we never store, and how to ask us to
remove something. It applies to the whole trip, the team and every ambassador.

## What we collect

- **Counts per stop:** how many people, merchants and ambassadors took part, sats
  circulated, transactions made. Numbers, not identities.
- **Ambassador reports** keyed by an **ambassador ID, not a name**. The map from ID
  to name lives in one private sheet only the core team holds.
- **Consent**, written or verbal, logged by date and stop, stored privately.

## What we publish

- City-level counts, the route, lessons, and episodes that have passed consent
  review, plus the budget by category.
- In authoritarian stops: **city-level counts, not merchant pins**. A map of which
  shops take Bitcoin is useful, but in some places it is also a target list.
- Location **after** a stop, never during. Upcoming dates and venues go out through
  the partner community the day before, and on the site only afterwards.

## What we never store or publish

- **Names, phone numbers or wallet addresses** of anyone onboarded, especially
  activists and journalists. Verification is a count plus a receipt the person
  chooses to share, nothing stored.
- Photos or video of anyone who did not consent. Faces are blurred on request. No
  uniformed officers. No border or airport footage.
- Hidden **metadata**: we strip EXIF (GPS, device, time) from every photo and video
  before it reaches the site or the archive.
- Raw footage of activists or journalists, unless they explicitly agree to a raw
  release. The edited episode can be CC BY; the raw stays out of the open archive.
- Keys, tokens, Sheet IDs or wallet seeds in any repository (see each repo's
  `SECURITY.md`).

## How to ask us to change or remove something

Email **bitkwaofficial@gmail.com**. Tell us the stop and the message, photo or
detail, and we will correct or remove it from anything we control, no questions
asked.

## Why this matters

HRF funds people who work in dangerous places. Protecting the people we teach is
not an afterthought, it is the work. This policy is linked from the site privacy
page and from the [consent form](./consent-form.md).
