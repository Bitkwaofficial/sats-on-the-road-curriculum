# Ambassador Playbook

An ambassador is a local person who learns Bitcoin well enough to teach it, and
keeps teaching after the vehicle leaves town. Ambassadors are the reason Sats On
The Road outlives a single visit. This playbook is how the program works.

See also: [stipend and verification](./stipend-and-verification.md) ·
[monthly report template](./monthly-report-template.md) ·
[country lead guide](./country-lead-guide.md) ·
[code of conduct](./code-of-conduct.md).

## What an ambassador does

- Teaches Bitcoin in their community, using this curriculum (full two days, a
  single day, or the [two-hour session](../curriculum/one-session)).
- Helps people set up a wallet and make their first real payment.
- Onboards local merchants to accept Bitcoin (see
  [merchant onboarding](../curriculum/day-2/session-9-merchant-onboarding)).
- Supports activists and journalists to receive funds safely (see
  [opsec](../safety/opsec-for-activists-and-journalists.md)).
- Reports what happened each month and joins the monthly call.

## The mission

Our priority is reach plus safety. We especially want ambassadors who can help the
people most exposed to financial censorship: **activists, journalists, and the
groups that fund them**. Teaching a market trader to accept sats matters. Helping a
reporter receive support that cannot be frozen matters too. Both are the mission.

## Who makes a good ambassador (selection criteria)

- Completed **Day 2** of the training, and did the hands-on parts themselves
  (set up self-custody, made real payments, onboarded at least one merchant in the
  role-play).
- Trusted and known in their community; can gather a room.
- Patient teacher: explains simply, does not show off, does not shame beginners.
- Takes safety and consent seriously.
- Reliable: will actually report and show up to the monthly call.
- Honest about money. Someone who pressures people, sells "schemes", or asks for
  anyone's seed phrase is disqualified immediately.

A local country lead nominates ambassadors; selection is confirmed with the SOTR
team. Target `[CONFIRM: number]` ambassadors per town.

## The two-day training

Ambassadors are trained with this curriculum:

- **Day 1 (learner day):** money, what Bitcoin is, wallet setup, Lightning, and a
  real market purchase. Everyone attends.
- **Day 2 (ambassador day):** deeper self-custody, privacy and opsec for activists
  and journalists, how to teach, how to onboard a merchant, and this commitment.

See the [curriculum](../curriculum) for every session.

## The commitment

By becoming an ambassador you agree to:

- Run at least `[CONFIRM: number]` sessions per month, or onboard at least
  `[CONFIRM: number]` people and `[CONFIRM: number]` merchants per month.
- Submit the [monthly report](./monthly-report-template.md) (ten lines, from a
  phone) by the `[CONFIRM: day]` of each month.
- Join the monthly call (below).
- Follow the [code of conduct](./code-of-conduct.md) and the
  [safety](../safety) rules.
- Protect people's privacy and never store or share personal data carelessly.

In return you receive the training, the materials, a place in the network and the
monthly call, and a stipend tied to verified work (see
[stipend and verification](./stipend-and-verification.md)).

## The monthly report

Ten lines, filled on a phone: what you ran, how many people and merchants, sats
circulated, one thing that worked, one problem. See the
[template](./monthly-report-template.md). Reports feed the
[dashboard](../data/dashboard.csv) and the country reports.

## The monthly call

A `[CONFIRM: length]` group call on `[CONFIRM: schedule, e.g. the first Saturday]`.
We share wins, solve problems together, agree what to fix in the curriculum, and
confirm the month's stipends. Missing calls without notice counts against the
commitment.

## What gets you removed

- Asking anyone for their seed phrase or private key, ever.
- Running a scam, a "double your Bitcoin" scheme, or pressuring people to buy.
- Mishandling people's safety, privacy or consent (for example, filming without
  consent, or exposing an activist's funds).
- Taking a stipend for work that did not happen (false reporting).
- Repeatedly going silent: no reports and no calls for `[CONFIRM: number]` months.
- Any breach of the [code of conduct](./code-of-conduct.md).

Removal means losing the stipend and the ambassador badge. Serious safety breaches
are removed immediately.

## The ambassador badge

The badge is earned by doing, not by a test: complete Day 2, set up self-custody,
make real payments, onboard a merchant, and run your first session. The short
quizzes in each session are for the badge, not for pass or fail.
