# Code of Conduct

This applies to everyone in Sats On The Road: the crew, country leads,
ambassadors, volunteers, and contributors to this repository. It also sets how we
treat the people we meet and the people in our photos and videos.

## Our promise

We teach people to control their own money, safely and with dignity. We are
guests in every town we visit. We leave people safer and better informed than we
found them, never worse.

## Do

- **Teach honestly.** Explain risks as clearly as benefits. Bitcoin's price can
  fall. Self-custody means responsibility.
- **Respect everyone.** All ages, genders, faiths, languages and income levels are
  welcome. Meet people where they are.
- **Protect privacy and consent.** Ask before filming. Offer face-blur. Use the
  [consent form](../safety/consent-form.md). Never expose anyone's funds, seed,
  or identity.
- **Keep people's money safe.** Teach self-custody properly. Help people write and
  protect their seed phrase.
- **Be patient.** No question is too basic. Never shame a beginner.
- **Follow local law and customs**, and the [safety protocol](../safety/team-safety-protocol.md).

## Do not

- **Never ask for anyone's seed phrase or private key.** There is no legitimate
  reason to. This is grounds for immediate removal.
- **No scams or schemes.** No "double your Bitcoin", no guaranteed returns, no
  pressure to buy, no affiliate hustles.
- **No financial advice.** We teach how Bitcoin works and how to use it safely, not
  what to buy or when.
- **No harassment or discrimination** of any kind.
- **No filming without consent**, and never film seed phrases, balances, police,
  soldiers or checkpoints.
- **No false reporting** to claim a stipend for work that did not happen.

## How we treat people in the footage

- Consent first, always. Blur on request, and when in doubt.
- Never portray anyone as poor, desperate or foolish for views.
- Remove someone's image if they later ask.
- Protect anyone whose safety could be at risk, especially activists and
  journalists.

Serious safety breaches lead to immediate removal.
