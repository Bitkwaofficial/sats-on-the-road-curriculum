# Monthly report template

Copy the ten lines below into a message and fill them in. It is built for your phone. No learner names, just counts. Honest estimates are fine.

```
1. Town:
2. Month:
3. Sessions run:
4. People taught:
5. Wallets set up:
6. Merchants onboarded:
7. Sats circulated (estimate):
8. One thing that worked:
9. One problem:
10. Link to photos / consent:
```

Notes:

- Lines 4 to 7 are counts and estimates. Count events, not people. Do not list anyone's name, number, or ID.
- Line 10 is a link to your proof folder (signed confirmations, merchant messages, signage photos with consent). See [stipend and verification](stipend-and-verification.md) for what counts as proof.
- Send it in by the monthly call so your verified work can be reviewed and paid.
