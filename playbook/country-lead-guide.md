# Country lead guide

This is for the local partner community hosting a Sats On The Road visit. You are the person on the ground who makes the 2 days work. This guide lists what to do **before**, **during**, and **after** the visit. Tick each item as you go.

The course is 2 days: Day 1 is the learner day, Day 2 is the ambassador day. See the [curriculum](../curriculum/README.md) for the full schedule.

## Before the visit

- [ ] **Confirm dates** with the touring team `[CONFIRM: visit dates]`.
- [ ] **Book a venue** for both days. It needs seating, shade or a roof, and reliable power for charging phones. Note the address and a backup venue.
- [ ] **Check power and internet.** Arrange a power strip or two, and know where the nearest reliable signal is. Have a mobile data plan as backup.
- [ ] **Send invites** to learners for Day 1 (traders, students, neighbours) and pick the likely ambassadors for Day 2. Aim for a realistic number the room can hold.
- [ ] **Translations ready.** Confirm which languages are needed and that the handouts and slides exist in them. Flag any gaps to the team early. See [translations](../translations/GLOSSARY.md).
- [ ] **Line up the market and merchants.** Day 1 ends with buying something real in sats. Talk to a nearby market or a few shops in advance so they expect it. Line up merchants who might get onboarded on Day 2.
- [ ] **Phones and SIMs.** Check that learners will have phones. Have a few spare phones or SIMs for people who need one, and know that `[primary wallet, e.g. Blink or Phoenix]` is the planned teaching wallet. For feature phones, know whether Machankura (Lightning over USSD) is an option locally `[CONFIRM per country]`.
- [ ] **Local currency float.** Have a small amount of `[local currency]` and a small amount of sats ready so the market exercise can actually happen. Keep it modest.
- [ ] **Safety and permissions.** Check whether you need permission to gather or to use the venue. Think about the safety of attendees, especially any activists or journalists, and choose a setting where people feel safe to attend.
- [ ] **Media consent plan.** Decide how photos will be handled. Prepare a simple way for people to agree or decline to be photographed. No photos of anyone who has not clearly agreed.
- [ ] **Assign a local point person** for each day who knows the venue and the community.

## During the visit

- [ ] **Welcome and set the tone.** Greet people, explain the 2 days in plain words, and make clear that nobody's name goes on any list.
- [ ] **Register by count, not by name.** Track how many people attend, not who they are.
- [ ] **Run Day 1 (learner day).** Support the facilitator with translation, power, and keeping time.
- [ ] **Run the market exercise.** Walk learners to the market to buy something real in sats. Keep it calm and friendly with the merchants you lined up.
- [ ] **Run Day 2 (ambassador day).** Help the subset who will go on to teach and onboard merchants.
- [ ] **Onboard merchants.** Support the merchant-onboarding session with the shops you prepared.
- [ ] **Handle media consent on the spot.** Only photograph people and stalls that have agreed. Capture signage photos (for example a "Bitcoin accepted here" sign) where the merchant is happy for it.
- [ ] **Keep people safe.** Watch for anything that makes attendees uncomfortable and adjust. Protect the privacy of activists and journalists.
- [ ] **Note the counts as you go.** People taught, wallets set up, merchants onboarded, rough sats circulated.

## After the visit

- [ ] **Confirm the counts** with the facilitator: people taught, wallets set up, merchants onboarded, sats circulated estimate. Count events, not people.
- [ ] **Gather the proof.** Signed confirmations, merchant messages, and signage photos with consent. See [stipend and verification](stipend-and-verification.md).
- [ ] **Store proof and consent safely.** Keep it in one place, with no personal names or IDs of learners.
- [ ] **Check in on new ambassadors.** Make sure they know the monthly report and call.
- [ ] **File the report.** Use the [monthly report template](monthly-report-template.md) so verified work can be reviewed and paid.
- [ ] **Share one thing that worked and one problem** so the next town learns from you. A country report is welcome, see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
- [ ] **Thank the venue, the market, and the merchants.** They make the next visit easier.

## A note on money

We teach how Bitcoin works and how to use it safely. Nothing here is financial advice, and Bitcoin's price can fall. Keep the local float modest and the message honest. ₿
