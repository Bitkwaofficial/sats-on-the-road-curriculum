# Country lead guide

This is for the local partner community hosting a Sats On The Road visit. You are the person on the ground who makes the visit work. Here is what to do before, during, and after.

## Six weeks before arrival

- Name one local lead; open a WhatsApp group with the SOTR team.
- Shortlist 10–12 ambassador candidates (mix of market people, students, two with activist or journalist contacts).
- List 15–25 local journalists, activists and civil society people for the activist session; agree who invites them in person.
- Find venues: a hall for the two-day training, a market association room for merchant training, a hall for the country workshop (about 80 people). SOTR pays; you advise on cost.
- Find a vetted vehicle rental operator with a driver; send a quote.
- Recommend a hotel: clean, secure parking, reliable power, near the markets.
- Send market days and the school calendar for the visit dates.
- Name two or three native speakers to review translations and interpret.
- Arrange permission letters or introductions for filming in markets and schools.
- Suggest 20–30 merchants who are already curious.

## During the visit

- Co-host every session; introduce the team in the local language.
- Help select the final 8 ambassadors.
- Host the country workshop on the last day.

## After the visit

- Host the monthly ambassador call for your country.
- Distribute the documentary episode through your own channels.
- Send a one-paragraph note to SOTR at 30 and 90 days: who is still active, which merchants still accept sats, what broke.
