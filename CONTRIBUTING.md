# Contributing to Sats On The Road

Thank you for helping build this open Bitcoin curriculum. Anyone can help, and you do not need to be a developer. You can do almost all of it from your phone.

## Ways to help

- **Fix a typo or small error.** Spotted a wrong word, a broken link, or a confusing sentence? Fix it.
- **Add or improve a translation.** We teach in many languages. If you speak one of the local languages, your help makes the course reach more people.
- **Propose a change to a session.** If something in a facilitator guide, slide set, handout, or exercise can be clearer or more useful, suggest it.
- **Add a country report.** After a visit, share what worked in your town so the next person learns from it.

## Edit from your phone, right on GitHub

You do not need any special software.

1. Open the file you want to change here on GitHub.
2. Tap the pencil (edit) icon.
3. Make your change in the box.
4. Scroll down and describe your change in one line.
5. Tap **Propose changes**. GitHub walks you through the rest.

That is it. You just opened a pull request.

## What is a pull request (PR)?

A pull request is simply you saying "here is a change I suggest." A maintainer reads it, may ask a question, and then merges it in. Nothing you propose goes live until someone reviews it, so please do not worry about breaking anything.

If you prefer to raise a problem instead of fixing it yourself, open an issue. We have ready-made templates for hosting the course in your town and for reporting a handout error.

## Bigger changes

For a large change, it helps to open an issue first so we can talk it through before you spend time on it. For small fixes, just go ahead.

## Be kind

This is a community. We expect everyone to follow our [code of conduct](playbook/code-of-conduct.md). Be patient, be welcoming, and assume good faith.

## A note on money

Nothing here is financial advice. We teach how Bitcoin works and how to use it safely. We do not predict prices, and Bitcoin's price can fall. Please keep contributions in that same honest spirit.
